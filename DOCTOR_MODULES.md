## 2. MODULE ARCHITECTURE & SUB-MODULE BREAKDOWN

The Doctor workstation is organized into five dedicated sub-modules:

### 2.1 Module 1: Consultation Desk (`overview`)
The primary two-column clinical command center.

- **Clinical KPI Metrics**:
  - **Assigned Patients Today**: Total scheduled and walk-in patients on the physician's appointment docket.
  - **Patients in Waiting Queue**: Current count of patients checked in and waiting in the lounge or consultation room.
  - **Completed Consultations**: Consultations successfully completed with electronic clinical documentation.
- **Left Column: Today's Patient Queue**:
  - Lists patients in arrival and token order.
  - **Fields**: Token #, Time Slot, Patient Name, Reason for Visit, Status Badge (`scheduled`, `checked_in`, `in_consultation`, `completed`).
  - **Actions**:
    - **"Call into Room"**: Transitions status to `in_consultation`, loading the patient's record into the active examination desk.
    - **"Finish Consultation"**: Finalizes the visit, marks status as `completed`, and clears the patient from the active queue.
- **Right Column: Active Consultation Workstation**:
  - **Patient Demographic & Safety Banner**: Full name, MRN, age, biological sex, blood group, and highlighted medical allergy alerts (e.g., Penicillin, NSAIDs).
  - **Nurse Triage Vitals Panel**: Displays intake vitals recorded by the triage nurse (Daniel Cho, RN) prior to the visit:
    - Systolic & Diastolic Blood Pressure (`mmHg`)
    - Resting Pulse Rate (`bpm`)
    - Body Temperature (`°C`)
    - Oxygen Saturation $\text{SpO}_2$ (`%`)
    - Calculated Body Mass Index (BMI) with clinical weight category
  - **Chief Complaints & Clinical Notes**:
    - Patient visit reasons and history of present illness.
    - Multi-line clinical notes editor for examination findings, auscultation, and follow-up guidance.
  - **Embedded Prescription & Lab Summaries**: Quick review of ongoing medications and active laboratory orders directly within the consultation desk.

---

### 2.2 Sub-Module 2: My Patients (`patients`)
Dedicated patient directory restricted to patients under the care of this physician.

- **Patient Search & Lookup**:
  - Real-time search by Patient Name, MRN, or chronic condition.
- **Assigned Patients Directory Table**:
  - **Fields**: Patient Name, MRN, Age/Gender, Blood Group, Medical Allergies, Chronic Conditions (e.g., Hypertension, Dyslipidemia), and Record Status.
- **Action Control**:
  - **"Review EMR"**: Loads the patient's full clinical file directly into the consultation desk.

---

### 2.3 Sub-Module 3: Write & Review Prescriptions (`prescriptions`)
Electronic Prescription (e-Rx) management and drug therapy builder.

- **Prescription Ledger**:
  - Displays all electronic prescriptions generated by this physician.
  - **Fields**: Prescription Number (e.g., `RX-2026-101`), Patient Name, Clinical Diagnosis, Medication Line Items, Prescription Date, and Pharmacy Dispensary Status (`pending`, `dispensed`).
- **Electronic Prescription Builder Modal**:
  - **Diagnosis Input**: Formal clinical indication (e.g., "Essential Hypertension", "Bacterial Pharyngitis").
  - **Medication Item Configuration**:
    - Medicine Name (e.g., Amlodipine Besylate, Amoxicillin, Atorvastatin)
    - Dosage & Strength (e.g., 5 mg, 500 mg, 20 mg)
    - Frequency (e.g., Once daily morning, Twice daily after meals, TID)
    - Duration (e.g., 14 days, 30 days, 90 days)
    - Specific Patient Instructions (e.g., "Take with food", "Do not take with grapefruit juice")
  - **"Add Medicine" Action**: Dynamic multi-item prescription builder allowing multiple drug regimens in a single order.
  - **"Issue & Send to Pharmacy" Action**: Digitally signs and routes the prescription directly to the clinic dispensary queue.

---

### 2.4 Sub-Module 4: Order Diagnostic Lab Tests (`lab_orders`)
Requisition portal for clinical pathology and laboratory investigations.

- **Diagnostic Orders Table**:
  - **Fields**: Investigation Name, Patient Name, Priority Badge (`STAT`, `URGENT`, `ROUTINE`), Requisition Date, Specimen Status, and Clinical Indication.
- **Diagnostic Order Modal**:
  - **Investigation Selection**: Dropdown covering CMP, HbA1c, Lipid Profile, Complete Blood Count (CBC), hs-CRP, Thyroid Panel (TSH), Urinalysis.
  - **Clinical Priority Triage**:
    - `Routine`: Standard diagnostic turnaround.
    - `Urgent`: Rapid processing within 4 hours.
    - `STAT`: Emergency processing requiring immediate lab notification.
  - **Clinical Indication / Reason**: Notes explaining the diagnostic rationale.
  - **"Dispatch Lab Order" Action**: Submits order directly to the Laboratory Technician worklist.

---

### 2.5 Sub-Module 5: Weekly Schedule & Timetable (`schedule`)
Duty roster, consultation hours, and booked appointment slots.

- **Physician Availability Overview**:
  - Duty Days: Monday through Friday
  - Consultation Timetable: 09:00 AM - 04:30 PM
  - Consultation Fee Tariff: $150.00 / session
- **Booked Appointment Slots Table**:
  - Chronological breakdown of today's time slots, patient names, visit categories (scheduled vs. walk-in), and current progress.

## 4. END-TO-END CROSS-ROLE WORKFLOW INTEGRATION

```
[Receptionist] (Checks in Patient)
       │
       v
  [Nurse] (Measures Vitals & BMI)
       │
       v
[Doctor Consultation Desk] (Examines Patient & Reviews Vitals)
       │
       ├─────────────────────────────────┐
       v                                 v
[Pharmacist] (Fulfills e-Rx)     [Lab Technician] (Processes Lab Order)
       │                                 │
       └────────────────┬────────────────┘
                        v
               [Accountant Billing]
```

1. **Intake Handover**: The Doctor does not need to re-measure vitals; Nurse Daniel's triage readings populate the consultation workstation automatically.
2. **Instant Dispensary Routing**: Prescriptions created by the Doctor appear in Pharmacist Maya's queue within milliseconds, complete with patient allergy warnings.
3. **Real-Time Lab Results**: Completed tests certified by Lab Tech Samuel automatically populate the Doctor's patient view without page reloading.

## 5. REGULATORY & CLINICAL STANDARDS

- **Allergy Cross-Checking**: Allergy alerts are prominently displayed during examination and prescription writing.
- **Prescription Integrity**: Prescriptions cannot be issued without a valid clinical diagnosis and defined dosing schedule.
- **Confidentiality**: Access is limited to assigned outpatient files in accordance with healthcare privacy regulations.

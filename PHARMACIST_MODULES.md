## 2. MODULE ARCHITECTURE & SUB-MODULE BREAKDOWN

The Pharmacist workstation features three core operational modules:

### 2.1 Module 1: Dispensary Overview (`overview`)
The central pharmacy dashboard for dispensary workload and inventory alerts.

- **Dispensary KPI Metrics**:
  - **Prescriptions in Queue**: Outstanding electronic prescriptions issued by clinic doctors awaiting fulfillment.
  - **Dispensed Prescriptions Today**: Count of verified prescriptions handed over to patients or nursing staff today.
  - **Low Stock Warnings**: Pharmaceutical items whose current inventory has dropped below the minimum reorder threshold.
- **Dispensary Workload Status**:
  - Terminal status indicator verifying the electronic dispensary is online and receiving doctor e-Rx dispatches.

---

### 2.2 Sub-Module 2: Prescription Orders (`prescriptions`)
Electronic Prescription (e-Rx) fulfillment, allergy verification, and dispensing queue.

- **Prescription Queue Directory**:
  - Displays all electronic prescriptions generated by attending physicians.
  - **Fields Displayed**:
    - **Prescription #**: e-Rx reference (e.g., `RX-2026-101`).
    - **Patient**: Full patient name and registered contact.
    - **Clinical Diagnosis**: Primary condition diagnosed by the doctor (e.g., "Hypertension Follow-Up - Stable").
    - **Prescribing Doctor**: Physician who ordered the medication regimen.
    - **Safety Allergy Check**: Real-time cross-referencing against the patient's known drug allergies (e.g., Penicillin, Sulfonamides, NSAIDs).
    - **Dispensary Status**: `pending` or `dispensed`.
- **Medication Items Breakdown**:
  - Drug Commercial & Generic Name (e.g., Amlodipine Besylate 5 mg).
  - Dosage Strength and Administration Frequency (e.g., Once daily in morning).
  - Prescribed Treatment Duration (e.g., 30 days).
  - Specific Doctor Instructions (e.g., "Take with food", "Take before bedtime").
- **Action Control: "Dispense Medication"**:
  - Single-click fulfillment button.
  - Validates drug safety, decrements stock from pharmacy inventory, marks item status as `dispensed`, and updates the patient's portal in real time.

---

### 2.3 Sub-Module 3: Medication Inventory (`inventory`)
Comprehensive pharmaceutical supply, batch control, and warehouse management.

- **Inventory Search & Filter**:
  - Search by Drug Name, Generic Chemical Name, or SKU identifier.
- **Pharmaceutical Catalog Table**:
  - **SKU & Drug Name**: Stock Keeping Unit (e.g., `MED-001`) and commercial medication name.
  - **Generic Name & Category**: Formulation category (`tablet`, `syrup`, `injection`, `consumable`, `equipment`).
  - **Batch Number & Expiry**: Manufacturing batch ID (e.g., `BAT-2024-089`) and expiration date (`YYYY-MM-DD`).
  - **Stock Levels**:
    - Current Units in Stock.
    - Minimum Buffer Level (e.g., Min: 50 units).
    - Automatic rose badge if current stock $\le$ minimum threshold.
  - **Storage Location**: Pharmacy shelf or cold storage location (e.g., "Shelf A-12", "Cold Storage B", "Dispensary Rack 3").
  - **Unit Price**: Standard retail unit cost per package.
- **Action Control: "+50 Units" (Quick Restock)**:
  - Rapid inventory replenishment button for clinical stock maintenance without complex purchase order paperwork.

## 4. END-TO-END CROSS-ROLE WORKFLOW INTEGRATION

```
[Attending Doctor] ──── (Issues e-Rx) ────> [Pharmacy Dispensary Queue]
                                                    │
                                            (Allergy Verification)
                                                    │
                                                    v
                                            [Dispense Medication]
                                                    │
        ┌───────────────────────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────────────────┐
        v                                                                                       v
[Patient Portal]                                                                        [Accountant Ledger]
Prescription status switches to                                                         Pharmacy medication charges
"Dispensed - Ready for Collection"                                                      billed to patient invoice
```

1. **Doctor Requisition -> Pharmacy Arrival**: Doctors submit e-prescriptions digitally; no paper slips are needed. The order appears in Pharmacist Maya's queue instantly.
2. **Safety Screen -> Pharmacist**: If a doctor accidentally prescribes a drug conflicting with a patient's documented allergy, the pharmacy queue flags an alert.
3. **Dispensing -> Patient Update**: Dispensing updates the patient portal immediately, showing that their prescription is ready for pickup.
4. **Dispensing -> Financial Clearance**: Drug sales link to the billing system for payment processing.

## 5. PHARMACEUTICAL COMPLIANCE & SAFETY STANDARDS

- **Batch Traceability**: Every dispensed unit tracks batch numbers and expiration dates to support drug recall audits.
- **Minimum Stock Safeguards**: Prevents stockouts of essential cardiovascular, antibiotic, and emergency medications through proactive threshold alerts.
- **Dosage Verification**: Requires clear dosage strength, duration, and instructions before fulfillment.
